This train pack contains a collection of trains used in Hong Kong's MTR network, past present and the near future. Each trains contains as accurate performace as possible while leaving signifcant leeway of train formations for flexible use. Thanks to beta 1.7.12 seperating tracks and trains, heavy rail trains has been catagorized by its irl division (Urban, Airport, and KCR).

This pack includes trains as follows:
M-Train
M-Train Disneyland
K-Train (Urban and Airport)
C-Train (Urban and Tuen Ma)
MLR Train
IKK Train (8 and 12 cars variant)
R-Train
Light Rail
Macau LRT
